Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Answer First is made by ScaleLabs AI. This policy says what the app does with information.

What stays on your phone

Your decks, your answers, your progress, your settings and your choice of locked apps are stored only on your phone. There is no account and the app has no server of its own. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it in Settings → Your data.

Access the app asks for, and why

  • iPhone: Screen Time access. Lets the app block the apps you choose. Apple gives the app only opaque tokens for those apps; it cannot see their names or your usage.
  • Android: usage access. Lets the app see which app is in front, so it can cover a locked one. This is checked on the phone and never leaves it.
  • Android: display over other apps. Lets the app show the lock screen over a locked app.
  • Notifications. Used for the lock's own messages. No marketing.

The app does not collect or send which apps you lock, open or use.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. To know whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records. It does not receive your name, email or anything you do in the app. RevenueCat also uses these purchase records to show the developer sales totals, never who bought. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

What the app does not do

No ads, no tracking across apps, no sale of data, and no analytics of what you do in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
