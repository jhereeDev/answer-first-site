Answer First locks the apps you choose. To open one, you answer a few questions first.

A locked app opens without questions

The lock needs the access you gave it during setup. If that access was turned off, the lock stops working.

  • iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → turn on Answer First.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Special app access → turn on Usage access and Display over other apps for Answer First. Also allow its notification, which keeps the lock running.

Then open Answer First once and check that your apps are still selected.

Cancel a subscription

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Answer First → Cancel.
  • Android: Play Store → profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Answer First → Cancel.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Restore a purchase

In the app, open Settings → Answer First Pro → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account that bought it.

Erase your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Erase it in Settings → Your data, or delete the app.
